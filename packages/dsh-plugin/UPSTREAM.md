# Upstream / 来源

This is an independent adaptation of [zenstory-ai/oh-story-dsh v0.1.26](https://github.com/zenstory-ai/oh-story-dsh/releases/tag/v0.1.26), based on the released Git tree at **ba5e6d9501ab2869db3aebdd194915dcc719b4e6**. The host entry, browser workbench, native hooks and four pinned knowledge packs originate there. Not an official ZenStory AI or DeepSeek release. The old Kaitu application is not linked or imported by the runtime.

The upstream MIT copyright (worldwonderer) remains unchanged in LICENSE. The original Kaitu project's MIT notice is retained separately in LICENSE.kaitu. Every bundled knowledge pack retains its original LICENSE or LICENSE.upstream and manifest; the pins are Oh Story 0.10.5, Drama Skills 0.12.0, NovelToGame 0.9.0 and video-recap-skills 0.7.1. Embedded third-party notices remain with their respective files. User writing and provider services are not relicensed by the plugin.

Kaitu changes: fork-specific package identity, an optional book-local creative agreement delivered to novel skills, a thin native-session launcher for Kaitu Explore, and a text-only staging tool that does not modify the source manuscript. The rebuild does not carry upstream publishing or credentialed automation workflows; the destination repository's original read-only check remains.
